Fitclub Awards 2022

This will be the holding page and will be continually updated as we get closer.

 

Right now, not exactly sure what will happen and the format, but will be a similar theme to last year and I’m open to suggestions.

 

So we’ll have:

– Awards (categories and voting to be determined)

– Guess who Maya dressed up (we’ll have to see how impressed we are with a person chosen at random for Maya to dress) – if she’s coming that is

– Clanger of the year – live voting on some of your top clangers. This can be gaffes caught on video, text message exchanges or simply an anecdote of something that happened between members of the group and we’ll take a live vote. 

– Auction – it was successful last year, so we’ll do it again this year. Send me your ideas via DM on what you’d like to auction off

– I’ll also reveal who that Turkish archer looks like. Does he look like a character from Ertugrul (or whatever it’s called) or from the cast of Robin Hood: Men in Tights

 

Any other suggestions welcome

We based the venue off the results we got from the survey to determine what people were comfortable paying.

You get fed, entertained, mocked and humiliated.

What more you want for £30? Jam on it?

What was the Rumi-gate scandal?

It’s too long to explain here. I was writing a summary on it but gave up half way.

In a nutshell, someone put a quote in Fitclub. Someone else thought it was attributed to Rumi.

Someone on the Sufi side took offence that they would think it’s Rumi and didn’t like a scholar being insulted.

The person who thought it was Rumi thought he was just a poet and not a scholar.

A bit of back and forth with people being accused on insulting a scholar and some misunderstandings led to someone abruptly leaving the group.

There, I finally summarised it. Not very well, but it is late and I have to be up in a few hours
